Success Story

From inconsistent outbound to a pipeline that runs itself.

How we built and now operate an autonomous AI sales engine for an early-stage B2B team — the whole story, from the problem to keeping it running.

AI Sales Agent Outbound automation Human-approved Managed ongoing
01

The Problem

A real product, early customers — and pipeline that was a coin flip.

The team had traction, but outbound lived entirely on the founder: sporadic bursts of cold email, follow-ups that never happened, and replies slipping through the cracks. The sending domain was quietly landing in spam, and there was no way to say what was actually working.

Founder-led sales had hit its ceiling — and buying more tools only added more to operate, not more pipeline.

02

The Diagnosis

The bottleneck wasn't the list or the tools — it was that nothing connected them.

We mapped the whole funnel and found the real constraint: no system tied the pieces together, and no one had the hours to run them by hand. Specifically:

  • No written ICP and no lead scoring — targets were picked by gut
  • No metered sending or domain warm-up — deliverability was at risk
  • Follow-ups that didn't stop when someone replied
  • Replies going unread and unrouted
  • A CRM drifting out of sync with reality
03

The Solution

One system that owns the motion end to end — with a human on the one decision that matters.

We built and deployed a full autonomous outbound engine. It runs the whole pipeline on a schedule, and the founder keeps only the approval decision: who gets contacted.

  • Lead research & ICP scoring, deduped against everyone already contacted
  • Automatic weekly selection of the highest-fit leads
  • A human approval gate — nothing sends without sign-off
  • Personalized, metered cold email that protects the domain
  • Domain & inbox warm-up on a schedule
  • Timed follow-ups that stop the instant someone replies
  • Reply detection every 30 minutes, with AI classifying every response by intent
  • Automatic routing — each lead moved across the CRM board with a note explaining why
  • Deliverability & data hygiene — bounce tracking and an enforced suppression list
  • The CRM as the single source of truth
04

Maintaining the Solution

It doesn't just get handed over — we operate it.

The engine runs on a schedule — find leads, pick the batch, dispatch, check replies every 30 minutes, warm up, and post a weekly scorecard — with safety controls so the failure mode is always "does nothing," never "blasts everyone." Every send, reply, and move is logged as a note on the lead, so the CRM tells the whole story on its own.

Each week it reports on itself — reply rate, bounce rate, conversions, and meetings created — so the value is visible, not assumed.

The result: a consistent outbound motion that runs without anyone tending it, replies handled within half an hour, follow-ups that stop cleanly the moment someone engages, and a funnel that's finally measurable.
